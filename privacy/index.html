Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

The short version. This site measures how many people open it and which garment they click. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It has no accounts, no forms, no newsletter and no way to contact you. It does send some technical information about your visit to Google Analytics, and the companies that host the site and serve its fonts see your IP address, which is unavoidable for any website.

What this covers

Two things: the website at yerinseo.com, and the private tool its owner uses to read statistics from the Yerin Seo YouTube channel and reply to comments on it.

What the website measures, precisely

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (property G-MQ8PVH2VNM) on the home page and on the three garment pages. It is configured without storage, so no analytics cookie is written and no identifier is kept on your device between visits. What is sent to Google on a page view is:

WhatExample
Your IP addressunavoidable in any request; Google derives an approximate location from it
Browser and devicebrowser name and version, platform, platform version, architecture, whether it is a mobile device, device model where the browser reports one
Screen size and language1280×720, en-us
Which page, and its titleyerinseo.com/jacket
Where you came fromthe referring address, when your browser sends one
Which garment you clickedan event named shop_click carrying jacket, leggings or shop

That is the whole list. There is no profile, no advertising audience, no cross-site tracking, no Google Signals, and nothing is combined with data from anywhere else. The privacy page and the terms page carry no analytics at all.

Why, and on what basis

One reason only: to know whether anybody clicks through to a garment. Without that the site cannot tell whether it works at all. The legal basis is the owner’s legitimate interest in knowing whether their own page is used, kept as narrow as that purpose allows: no cookies, no device identifier, no persistence, no marketing.

If you would rather not be counted, any browser setting or extension that blocks Google Analytics will stop it completely, and the site works exactly the same without it. Nothing here is required for the page to function.

Turning the measurement off

One click, no account, free, and it sticks on this browser.

This stores one small preference on your own device so the choice is remembered. Nothing else is stored, and the preference is never sent anywhere.

Leaving this site

Each garment page has a button to the seller’s own shop. When you use it, the shop is told only that you arrived from yerinseo.com, and from that point their privacy policy applies and not this one. Shops of that kind commonly set their own cookies and run their own analytics and advertising tools. The same is true of the Instagram, TikTok and YouTube links.

Commission

No link on this site earns anything at present. There is no affiliate programme, no tracking parameter on any outbound link, and no arrangement with any brand. If that ever changes, the link will say so where you can see it and this page will be updated.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask what is held about you, to have it corrected or erased, to object to it being processed, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Being honest about the limits: because nothing here identifies you, stores anything on your device, or lets the owner contact you, there is normally no record that could be found and handed to you even if you asked. Ask anyway if you want to — the contact channel is below — and you will get a straight answer about what exists.

The YouTube tool

The owner of the Yerin Seo channel runs a private tool on their own computer that uses Google’s YouTube APIs. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.

It is authorised for two things, both on the owner’s own channel:

  • YouTube Analytics, read only. Aggregate statistics for the channel: view counts, traffic sources, subscriber numbers, age and country bands. These are summaries and do not identify individual viewers.
  • YouTube Data. Reading the public comments left on the channel’s own videos, and posting replies to them as the channel.

The credential Google issues is stored on the owner’s own computer and is used only to call Google. Channel statistics and comment text are kept locally for writing replies and tracking how the channel is doing.

Nothing read through these APIs is sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to build a profile of anyone. No human other than the channel owner reads it. One transfer does happen and is worth stating plainly: the owner drafts replies to comments with the help of an AI assistant, so the text of a public comment being replied to is sent to that assistant’s provider for that purpose and nothing else. Aggregate channel statistics are not. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping it, and deleting it

Google holds the analytics data under the retention period set on the property and deletes it when that expires. Locally stored channel statistics and comment records are kept only as long as they are useful. The Google authorisation for the YouTube tool can be withdrawn at any time from the owner’s Google Account security settings, which immediately ends its access.

This site is not directed at children and nothing here asks anyone their age, because there is nothing to sign up to and no way to submit anything.
