Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

This policy covers two things: the website at yerinseo.com, and the private tool its owner uses to read statistics from the Yerin Seo YouTube channel and to reply to comments on it.

The website

There is nothing to sign in to here. The site is a set of static files. It has no accounts, no forms, no shopping cart, no analytics, and it sets no cookies of its own.

Two services are loaded from outside to render the page as designed:

  • Google Fonts and Fontshare, for the typefaces. Loading a file from either one reveals your IP address and browser to that provider, the same as visiting any page that uses them. Neither is given any other information about you.

Links to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube leave this site. What those platforms collect is covered by their own policies, not this one.

Links that earn a commission

Some product links on this site may earn a commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. Following such a link takes you to the retailer, and the retailer may set a cookie to record that the visit came from here. The retailer tells the owner how many sales resulted. It does not tell the owner who you are.

The YouTube tool

The owner of the Yerin Seo channel runs a private tool on their own computer that uses Google's YouTube APIs. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and no one other than the channel owner signs in to it.

It is authorised for two things, both on the owner's own channel:

  • YouTube Analytics, read only. Aggregate statistics for the channel: view counts, traffic sources, subscriber numbers, age and country bands. These are summaries. They do not identify individual viewers.
  • YouTube Data. Reading the public comments left on the channel's own videos, and posting replies to them as the channel.

The access credential Google issues is stored in a file on the owner's own computer and is used only to call Google. Channel statistics and comment text are kept locally for the purpose of writing replies and tracking how the channel is doing.

Nothing read through these APIs is sold, rented, shared with any third party, used for advertising, or used to build a profile of anyone. No human other than the channel owner reads it. It is not transferred to any other service.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping it, and deleting it

Locally stored statistics and comment records are kept only as long as they are useful and are deleted when they are not. The Google authorisation can be withdrawn at any time by the owner from their Google Account's security settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a direct message to @yerinseo.kr on Instagram.
