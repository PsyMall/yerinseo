Terms, and how the images work

Last updated 12 September 2026

The pictures here are generated, not photographed

Yerin Seo is a digital character. Every image and every video on this site and on her Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts is made with generative AI. She is not a real person and none of it is a photograph of a real garment on a real body.

What decides what a product actually looks like

The photographs on the seller’s own product page are what the product actually is. An image here shows how a piece has been interpreted by an AI model, and a generated image will not reproduce a real garment exactly. Colour, weave, print scale, hardware and proportion can all come out slightly different from the real thing, and screens differ on top of that.

So before buying anything: look at the seller’s pictures, sizing and description, and treat those as definitive. Where they disagree with anything shown here, the seller is right and this site is wrong.

Every shelf item has a page on this site carrying the seller’s own description and a button that opens their product page, for exactly this reason. That page counts the click and stores nothing on your device; what it measures is set out in the privacy policy.

Stock, price and availability are not undertaken here. Anything shown on this site was copied from the seller’s page at the time it was written and can be out of date within the hour. Whether a piece exists, in your size, at that price, is answered only on the seller’s own page at the moment you look.

We are not the seller

Nothing is sold from this site. There is no cart, no checkout and no order. The shelf is a set of links to independent brands, and the sale, the price, the stock, the sizing, the shipping, the returns and any warranty are entirely between you and that brand under their own terms.

Prices and availability shown here are whatever was true when the page was written and change without notice. The seller’s page is the current one.

Brands, and permission

Brands named here are named as credit, so that the maker of a piece can be found and paid. A brand appearing on this site does not mean it endorses, sponsors or has reviewed anything here, and their names and trade marks remain theirs. If you are a brand and you would rather not appear, say so and the item comes off.

Commission

Some links may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Any link that does is marked as such on the shelf. Links that earn nothing are marked too.

Accuracy, and the limits of it

Product details are copied from the seller’s own page in good faith and can go out of date or be wrong. Nothing here is advice about fit, safety, materials or suitability for any purpose. Use your own judgement, and the seller’s information, before buying.

Accessibility

This statement is made voluntarily. On the law applicable to this site, no accessibility statement is required of it: the Israeli service-accessibility regulations bind a provider of a public service and exempt a party below the turnover threshold they specify; Directive (EU) 2019/882 applies to e-commerce services provided with a view to concluding a consumer contract, which this site does not do, and separately exempts microenterprises providing services; and United Kingdom law imposes no conformance standard and no statement obligation on a site of this character.

Conformance. The site is intended to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. The Israeli standard IS 5568 part 1 (September 2023) declares itself identical to WCAG 2.0, and conformance at that level is accordingly also intended.

Measurement. All six pages of this site were tested on 12 September 2026 with axe-core version 4.10.2, against the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 rule sets at Levels A and AA, at a viewport of 390 by 844 pixels. No violation was reported on any page. Automated testing does not establish conformance on its own; it establishes the absence of the defects such testing is capable of detecting.

Known limitations. The typefaces are loaded from third-party providers and a failure at either provider causes the page to render in a substitute typeface. Each photograph carries a text alternative stating both its content and that the image was generated by AI.

Reporting a barrier. A person encountering an accessibility barrier on this site is invited to report it through the contact channel below. A report will be answered, and a barrier capable of being removed will be removed.
