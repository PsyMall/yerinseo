Terms, and how the images work

Last updated 12 September 2026

The pictures here are generated, not photographed

Yerin Seo is a digital character. Every image and every video on this site and on her Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts is made with generative AI. She is not a real person and none of it is a photograph of a real garment on a real body.

What decides what a product actually looks like

The photographs on the seller’s own product page are what the product actually is. An image here shows how a piece has been interpreted by an AI model, and a generated image will not reproduce a real garment exactly. Colour, weave, print scale, hardware and proportion can all come out slightly different from the real thing, and screens differ on top of that.

So before buying anything: look at the seller’s pictures, sizing and description, and treat those as definitive. Where they disagree with anything shown here, the seller is right and this site is wrong.

Every shelf item links straight to the seller’s own page for exactly this reason.

We are not the seller

Nothing is sold from this site. There is no cart, no checkout and no order. The shelf is a set of links to independent brands, and the sale, the price, the stock, the sizing, the shipping, the returns and any warranty are entirely between you and that brand under their own terms.

Prices and availability shown here are whatever was true when the page was written and change without notice. The seller’s page is the current one.

Brands, and permission

Brands named here are named as credit, so that the maker of a piece can be found and paid. A brand appearing on this site does not mean it endorses, sponsors or has reviewed anything here, and their names and trade marks remain theirs. If you are a brand and you would rather not appear, say so and the item comes off.

Commission

Some links may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Any link that does is marked as such on the shelf. Links that earn nothing are marked too.

Accuracy, and the limits of it

Product details are copied from the seller’s own page in good faith and can go out of date or be wrong. Nothing here is advice about fit, safety, materials or suitability for any purpose. Use your own judgement, and the seller’s information, before buying.
